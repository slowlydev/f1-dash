# Screen pocket test print

A cut-out of the wheel around the screen (154 x 83 x 41 mm) plus the rear access plate, so the fit can be
checked without printing the whole wheel. No paint or colour is set; print everything in one grey or black filament.

- `screen_pocket_test_print.3mf`: both parts laid out on one plate (P1S bed is 256 x 256 mm).
- `wheel_screen_pocket_test.stl` is printed front face down. `screen_access_plate_test.stl` is printed rear face down.
  Supports are needed under the plate's thin upper cover and the USB-C tunnel roof; everything else prints without.

## What to check

1. The module drops into the pocket from the back and sits flat against the front lip.
2. The screen's visible area lines up with the 90.4 x 51 mm window.
3. The plate's four posts touch the module's corner tabs, and M2.5 x 14 mm screws reach the inserts.
4. The USB-C plug fits through the side tunnel and reaches the port.
5. The M3 heat-set inserts (4.2 mm holes, 6 mm deep) fit, and the plate seats with M3 x 10 mm screws.
6. A short LED strip fits in the top channel (6 mm wide).

Parts needed: 4 x M2.5 x 14 mm screws, 4 x M3 x 10 mm screws, 4 x M3 heat-set inserts.
